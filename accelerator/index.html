Why three months and not one

One month of posting is not data, it is a mood. Thirty days gives you a handful of posts, one of which did fine, and no way to know whether that was the topic, the hook, the day, or luck.

Three months gives you enough volume that the pattern shows up. It also gives us two rounds of adjusting the plan based on your real numbers instead of our assumptions. The first month is setup. The value compounds in months two and three. That is why we do not sell it one month at a time, and why we would rather turn you down than take $600 for a plan you abandon in week three.
