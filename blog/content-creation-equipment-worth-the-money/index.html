Frequently asked questions

What equipment do you need to start content creation?

Less than you think. A camera that shoots clean vertical video (a modern phone counts), a wireless microphone so your audio is clear, and one soft light source. Good audio and good light do more for how professional you look than an expensive camera body.

Is expensive camera gear worth it for social media content?

Only in the right order. Spend first on audio and lighting — those fix the problems viewers notice fastest. A mid-range camera with a great mic and soft light beats a top-tier camera with bad sound and harsh light every time. Buy the expensive body last.

What's the best budget microphone for content creators?

A compact wireless lavalier mic is the single highest-impact upgrade for most creators. It clips on, records a clean backup track, and removes the biggest giveaway of amateur content: thin, echoey audio.
