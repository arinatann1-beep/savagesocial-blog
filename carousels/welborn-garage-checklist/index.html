Look & Listen
✅ DO #1
Once a month, watch it open. Grinding, popping or jerking is your early warning — far cheaper to fix now than later.

Lube It Right
✅ DO #2
Hit the rollers, hinges & springs with silicone or white-lithium spray. Skip the WD-40 — it's a degreaser, not a lubricant.

The Tracks
✅ DO #3
Wipe them clean with a rag — don't grease them. Grease catches dust and gums up the whole door.

✅ DO #4
