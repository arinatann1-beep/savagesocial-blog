Slide 3 — The WD-40 myth
@1GARAGEDOORDOCTOR  |  GARAGE DOOR DOCTOR
→
The WD-40 Myth
🚫 Don't
Spray WD-40 on your tracks or rollers — it's a degreaser that strips lubrication and attracts grime
✅ Do
Use a silicone or white-lithium garage-door lube on the rollers, hinges & springs
Slide 4 — The tracks
@1GARAGEDOORDOCTOR  |  GARAGE DOOR DOCTOR
→
The Tracks
🚫 Don't
Grease the tracks — grease catches dust and dirt and gums up the whole door
✅ Do
Wipe the tracks clean with a rag. Lube the moving parts — not the track
Slide 5 — The springs
@1GARAGEDOORDOCTOR  |  GARAGE DOOR DOCTOR
→
The Springs
🚫 Don't
Touch the torsion springs — they're under extreme tension and can seriously hurt you
✅ Do
Leave springs, cables & opener-force settings to a trained pro
Slide 6 — The 2-minute test
@1GARAGEDOORDOCTOR  |  GARAGE DOOR DOCTOR
→
The 2-Min Test
✅ Do
Once a month, pull the release and lift the door halfway — a healthy door stays put
🚫 Don't
Keep using it if it slams, creeps or won't hold — that's a spring or balance issue
