## Slide copy
1. **Cover** — "5 things to do for your garage door" + 2 min/month saves a $400 repair.
2. **Do #1 · Look & Listen** — watch it open monthly; grinding/popping = early warning.
3. **Do #2 · Lube It Right** — silicone / white-lithium on rollers, hinges, springs; skip WD-40.
4. **Do #3 · The Tracks** — wipe clean, don't grease.
5. **Do #4 · Test The Balance** — lift halfway, it should stay put.
6. **Do #5 · Safety Reverse** — broom test; it should reverse instantly.
